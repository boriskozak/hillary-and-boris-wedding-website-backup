DETAILS

Attire

Attire is Black Tie Optional / Red Carpet Ready.

For the ladies: a long dress (preferred), dressy separates, or a formal cocktail dress. If you're interested in renting a dress, visit Rent the Runway and get $30 off.

For the men: a tuxedo or a formal dark suit, white shirt, and bowtie preferred. If you're interested in renting a tux, visit The Black Tux.

Location and Directions

The ceremony, cocktail hour and reception will be held at The Oaks Room at Calamigos Ranch in Malibu, California.

Shuttles will be provided to and from The Sheraton Agoura Hills and The Residence Inn by Marriott at Westlake Village. Complimentary valet parking will be provided for guests driving to the venue. Finding the location can be a bit tricky.

From Orange County / LA

Take the 405 N to the 101 N. Take the Kanan Road exit (Exit 36). When exiting the freeway, turn left onto Kanan Dume Road. Take Kanan Dume Road to Mulholland Hwy. Turn right on Mulholland Hwy. The entrance to Calamigos will be on the left.

Alternate route

Take the 405 N to the 10 W. Continue onto CA-1 N (Pacific Coast Highway). Turn right on Kanan Dume Road. Turn right on Mulholland Hwy. The entrance to Calamigos will be on the left.
